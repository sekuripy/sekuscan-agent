# Security policy

Please report a vulnerability in the agent, or in SekuScan generally, to
**security@sekuripy.hr**. Include what you found, how to reproduce it and
which version you used (`pip show sekuscan-agent`).

We acknowledge reports within two business days and keep you informed until
the issue is fixed. Please do not disclose publicly until a fix is released.
See also <https://sekuscan.com/.well-known/security.txt>.
